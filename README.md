# Velum

A native Android camera for the Galaxy S24 Ultra (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, CameraX + Camera2). Everything runs on
the phone: no uploads, analytics or ads.

What it does: photo (JPEG, RAW+JPEG, Ultra HDR, XPan), Night and Portrait through the camera's own extensions,
Portrait at 3x/5x with depth blur, Pro photo and video, Macro, Astro, Long exposure, Best shot, Clone, Motion photo,
video up to 4K with HLG, Movie (cinematic depth of field with automatic subject and tracking), Film, Vlog, Effects,
Dual video, Slow motion 120/240 fps, Horizon lock, and a look system (Essentials, brand-inspired colour, film, mono,
cinema, the slay LUTs, custom looks, .cube import, QR presets) that renders the same in the viewfinder, saved photos
and recorded video.
