## Facts that decide features
- **4K 60 fps**: camera 0 advertises 3840×2160 PRIVATE with a 33.3 ms minimum frame duration (30 fps max) to public clients. Largest 16:9 stream at 60 fps is 1920×1080. Samsung Camera's 4K60 uses Samsung-private vendor sessions not available to other apps.
- **200 MP**: no camera reports ULTRA_HIGH_RESOLUTION_SENSOR; maximum-resolution arrays equal the binned 4080×3060.
- **Telephoto access**: physical sensors 6 (3×, 7.9 mm f/2.4) and 7 (5×, 18.6 mm f/3.4) are readable and can be pinned per stream; on the logical camera the HAL chooses the sensor itself (it crops the main sensor at 3× in dim/close scenes).
- **Shutter priority / Kelvin**: no AE priority modes and no CCT colour-correction mode → implemented app-side (closed-loop ISO; daylight-calibrated RGGB gains).
- **Concurrent cameras**: the HAL lists pairs {0,1} and {0,3}; CameraX binds back+front for Dual video.
- **High speed**: Camera2 lists 1280×720 and 1920×1080 at 120/240 fps; CameraX reports no high-speed profile, so Slow motion uses a Camera2 constrained high-speed session.
- **Scene modes**: FACE_PRIORITY and HDR are offered; face detection SIMPLE is supported.
